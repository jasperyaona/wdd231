NP Membership

Free for registered non-profit organizations serving the community.

  • Listing in the online business directory
  • Invitations to chamber networking events
  • Access to volunteer and partnership opportunities

Bronze Membership

A low-cost way for new and small businesses to get involved.

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Business directory listing with logo
  • One discounted event ticket per quarter

Silver Membership

More visibility and savings for growing businesses.

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Priority placement in the business directory
  • Discounted rates on chamber training workshops
  • Two free event tickets per quarter

Gold Membership

The fullest set of benefits for established businesses.

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Featured member spotlight on the chamber home page
  • Free booth at the chamber's annual expo
  • Unlimited event tickets for staff
